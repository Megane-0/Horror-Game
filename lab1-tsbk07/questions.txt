3. What is the purpose of the "in", "out" and "uniform" modifiers? Be more specific than saying “input” and “output”.
    - uniform: a read-only modifiers for global variables like matrixes that should not be changed.
    - in: for vertexes these are variables that can be different for different vertexes, for fragments its a value for a given vertexes in the previous stage.
    - out: for vertexes output passes data to fragment shader. For fragments out represents the final value for that pixel.

What is the output of the fragment shader?
    - typically a pixel-value so a vector4 of (RGBA)
